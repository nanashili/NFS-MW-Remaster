# VehicleAudio authoring guide

Attach one `VehicleAudio` component to the vehicle root. Select an decoded `VehicleSensoryProfile` from `Assets/NfsMw/Content/Vehicles/Street/<brand>/<model>/Sound/Profiles/<VEHICLE ID>.asset` (93 stock variants), or enable the explicit `MostWantedVehicleAudio` bank override. For example, the BMW race car uses `Assets/NfsMw/Content/Vehicles/Street/BMW/M3 GTR/Sound/Profiles/BMWM3GTR.asset`. The component discovers its `VehicleController` telemetry and scene audio world at startup; no second audio component or manually configured telemetry proxy is needed.

The **Author mix** foldout exposes the master controls and all 21 `VehicleAudioChannel` entries. Each channel has gain, pitch, tempo, pan, low-pass and high-pass controls. Gain is `0..2`, pitch and tempo are `0.5..2`, so a layered change can combine to a `0.25..4` playback factor. For AEMS recordings, tempo changes independent authored timeline duration while pitch changes grain playback pitch. GIN is sustained and RPM-addressed: tempo changes its grain/envelope cadence and does not turn it into a finite-duration clip or change its RPM address.

Additional sounds accept either a custom PCM `AudioClip` or a decoded `AemsAudioBank`. Choose a recovered interface by its actual name; the inspector fills the parameter slots from that interface's declared count. Add typed bindings for RPM, load, speed, throttle, brake, gear, slip, nitrous, horn, siren and other available signals, and choose the trigger that owns playback: manual, startup, shifts, limiter, overrun, nitrous, impact, horn, siren, constant, speed, load, RPM, wheel slip or scrape.

Use **Open VehicleAudio Rehearsal** from the component inspector, or **NFS MW Remaster → Audio → Vehicle Rehearsal**. Enter Play Mode with the saved vehicle selected, then adjust RPM, load, speed, gear, throttle, brake, nitrous, wheel contact, surface and slip. Horn, siren, `Send frame now` and `Submit impact` exercise the production component and recovered bank events. Mix edits are live through the existing `Mix` reference; use **Rebuild audio content** after changing profile, bank or attached sound structure. The window restores manual-input ownership and clears horn/siren when closed or retargeted.

To tune before a vehicle model exists, create an empty GameObject, add `VehicleAudio`, assign its decoded profile, and open the rehearsal window. Ensure the scene has one enabled `AudioListener` near that object, then enter Play Mode. The rehearsal supplies telemetry directly; no mesh or physics controller is needed.

For gameplay events, call `Play("your-sound-id")`, `SetHorn(true/false)` or `SetSiren(true/false)` on the component. A manual sound can start before driving telemetry exists. Continuous bank attachments stop advancing while their trigger is inactive and restart their recovered graph on reactivation. Keep looping behavior in the decoded bank's own program; the loop checkbox and frame bounds apply to attached PCM clips.

Playback uses one pooled spatial voice per car while the runtime reports its independent active layers. This authoring path does not claim bit-perfect parity with the original executable or audible QA; validate listening quality against the intended build and reference capture separately.
